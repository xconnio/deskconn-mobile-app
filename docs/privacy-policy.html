Privacy Policy — Deskconn

Last updated: September 14, 2026 (v1)

Deskconn is a remote desktop app by xconnio that lets you access your own desktop's files, terminal, and system resources from your phone.

To use the app, we collect your email, username, and password to create your account, and send a one-time code (OTP) to your email to verify sign-in. We don't use any ads or analytics in the app, and we don't sell or share your data with third parties.

You can delete your account anytime from inside the app (Account → Delete account).

Questions? Contact us at admin@xconn.io.
